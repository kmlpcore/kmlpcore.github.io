Overview

An existing system, made cleaner and easier to use, then installed on location.

The Visitor Management System (VMS) is software used at Maharashtra Police locations. I improved the frontend of the existing system and deployed it on-site across four police zones.

The brief

I was not starting from a blank page. The software already existed and was already doing its job. My task was to improve how the interface looks and feels for the people who use it, and to get it running properly on location.

The core questionHow do you make a working system easier to use without disturbing what already works?

Design approach

I focused on the initial screens, where people first meet the software, and changed three things.

Calmer colour

I simplified the colour scheme so the interface feels consistent and restrained.

Deployment

The software was successfully deployed and configured on-site across four police zones, covering 6+ locations. The deployment involved setting up and configuring the required machines, installing the software, conducting on-site testing with police personnel, and ensuring the system functioned as expected. I also facilitated staff onboarding by explaining the software's features and workflow, guiding them through its usage, and preparing a demonstration video as a reference for future use.

Outcome

The VMS received positive feedback from staff for its intuitive interface and ease of use. The multilingual translation feature further improved accessibility, helping visitors with limited literacy navigate the registration process independently. Its successful implementation across multiple police zones demonstrated the system's effectiveness in streamlining visitor management.

The resultCalmer colour + clearer type + a cleaner layout = an interface that is easier to use, live across four zones.

What I learned

Working on software for a public institution taught me that clarity and reliability matter more than novelty. An interface only counts once it runs properly on the real machine, in the real room, for the people who depend on it. Deployment is part of the product.
